Privacy Policy – Vaulty

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Vaulty is a private file vault application developed by ArkDevX. It helps users protect their private photos, videos, documents, audio files, and other supported files on their devices. Your privacy and security are important to us.

Information Collection

Vaulty does not collect, transmit, sell, or share users' private files or personal information through its own servers.

Files imported into Vaulty are stored locally on the user's device. Vaulty does not require users to create an online account to use its core vault functionality.

Permissions

Vaulty may request limited permissions required for its functionality:

  • Biometric/Fingerprint Permission: Used to unlock the private vault securely through fingerprint authentication on supported devices.
  • Camera Permission: Used by the Intruder Selfie feature to capture a photo after three consecutive incorrect PIN attempts. Captured photos are stored locally within the app and are not uploaded to or shared with external servers.
  • Storage and Media Permissions: Used to access, import, manage, and restore supported photos, videos, documents, audio files, and other supported files.
  • Notification Permission: Used for app notifications or security alerts, if enabled.

These permissions are used for Vaulty's functionality and are not intended for tracking or collecting personal information.

Data Security

Vaulty is designed to protect users' private files using local device security methods such as PIN and biometric authentication.

Vaulty does not upload users' private files to its own servers.

Security Features

Vaulty provides additional security features to help users protect their private files:

  • Fingerprint Lock: Allows users to unlock their private vault using fingerprint authentication on supported devices. Biometric authentication is handled through the device's built-in security system. Vaulty does not access or store users' actual fingerprint data.
  • Fake PIN: Allows users to enter a separate PIN to open a dummy vault while keeping their actual private files hidden.
  • Intruder Selfie: After three consecutive incorrect PIN attempts, Vaulty captures a photo using the device camera. The captured photo is stored locally within the app and is not uploaded to or shared with external servers.

File Recovery and Recycle Bin

Vaulty provides file management and recovery features to help users manage their private files.

  • File Recovery: Allows users to access and restore older files that are available through the app's recovery functionality.
  • Recycle Bin: Allows users to view and restore accidentally deleted files when they are still available in the Recycle Bin.

These features operate using files stored locally on the user's device. Files are not uploaded to external servers for recovery.

Data Backup and Recovery

Vaulty stores users' private files locally on the device and does not require cloud backup, online synchronization, or an online account.

One of Vaulty's important features is uninstall data persistence. Private files stored in Vaulty's supported storage location remain available even if the app is accidentally uninstalled and subsequently reinstalled on the same device.

This feature helps users retain access to their protected files without requiring an online backup or account recovery service.

However, files may be permanently lost if they are manually deleted, the device is reset, the storage location is formatted, or the files are otherwise removed from the device.

Users are responsible for protecting their devices and avoiding permanent deletion of their stored files.

Third-Party Services

Vaulty does not sell or share users' private files with third parties.

Vaulty does not use online tracking services or analytics to collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

Vaulty is not intended for children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
